# SA Estimation Norms — Bill of Quantities (Materials) Reference

Residential building BOQ. Every factor the Plans/BOQ agent is allowed to use for
material quantities. Sources: SANS 227, SANS 10400 (Part A-G), Standard System
of Measuring Building Work (ASAQS), Concrete Society of Southern Africa
leaflets, AfriSam building/plastering guides, Clay Brick Association,
PPC/Ozcem, Macsteel, IBR World, RMCS/SA steel suppliers.

Measurement: metric (mm/m/m²/m³/No). Net measure, dimensions rounded per ASAQS
to nearest 10 mm. Wastage added at the stated %.

## 1. Bricks & blocks

| Unit | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard brick SANS 227 | 222 × 106 × 73 mm work size | 10 mm joints; coordinating 232×116×83 |
| Single-skin (110 mm) wall | **52 bricks/m²** | stretcher bond (industry 50–53) |
| Double-skin (220 mm) wall | **104 bricks/m²** | |
| Maxi/cement brick 290×140×90 | ~29 bricks/m² single skin | confirm brick type |
| Hollow concrete block 390×190×140 | ~12.5 blocks/m² | "14 MPa block" |
| Bricks wastage | **10 %** | 5 % experienced layer; 15 % complex/curved |
| Mortar mix | **1:6** cement:building sand (Class II) | standard; **1:4** load-bearing / below DPC / exposed |

Mortar per brick (SANS/CBA): ~0.00037 m³ wet mortar per standard brick @10 mm
joint → **0.37 m³ per 1 000 bricks**.

Cement & sand per 1 000 bricks (Concrete Society SA / AfriSam figures):
- 1:6 mix → **3 × 50 kg bags cement + ≈0.55–0.60 m³ building sand**
- 1:4 mix → **≈4–5 × 50 kg bags cement + ≈0.50–0.55 m³ sand**

Notes: 50 kg cement bag = ≈0.033 m³ loose; sand bulks when damp (measure by
volume). 1 wheelbarrow ≈ 2 bags of cement volume.

## 2. Concrete mixes (SANS 10400) — quantities per 1 m³ compacted

Mix assumes damp-bulked sand + 50 kg OPC bags.

| Strength | Use | Cement (50 kg) | Sand (m³) | Stone (13/19 mm, m³) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 MPa (lean/blinding) | blinding, baling | 4 bags | 0.80 | 0.80 |
| **15 MPa** | unreinforced footings, boundary/retaining walls | **5.5 bags** | **0.75** | **0.75** |
| **25 MPa** | reinforced foundations, house slabs, driveways, garages | **7 bags** | **0.70** | **0.70** |
| **30 MPa** | beams, suspended/precast floors, heavy duty | **10 bags** | **0.65** | **0.65** |

- Default wastage: **+10 %** to all concrete.
- Water: ≈25 L/bag cement (medium workability).
- Itemize per element (blinding, footings, ground/suspended slab, columns, lintel
  topping, screed) — do not lump.

## 3. Reinforcing steel & mesh

SA high-tensile (Y, 450 MPa) and mild (R, 250 MPa). Mass = d²/162 kg/m:

| Bar | kg/m |
|---|---|
| R6 | 0.222 |
| R8 | 0.395 |
| Y10 | 0.617 |
| Y12 | 0.888 |
| Y16 | 1.578 |
| Y20 | 2.466 |
| Y25 | 3.854 |

- Stock lengths: **6 m and 12 m**. Order whole bars; sum cut lengths incl. laps,
  divide by stock length, round up.
- Lap: ≈40 × bar Ø (Y bars). Cover: footings/slab to earth 50 mm; inside 20–25 mm;
  external 30–40 mm.
- Typical reinforcement mass per m³ concrete (sanity check): strip/pad footings
  20–40 kg/m³; slabs 60–110 kg/m³; beams/columns 80–150 kg/m³.
- Slab rule of thumb: domestic single-layer ≈6–10 kg per m² of slab.
- Welded mesh (SA): A142 (6 mm@200), A193 (7 mm@200), A252 (8 mm@200),
  A393 (10 mm@200). Sheet 6 m × 2.4 m = 14.4 m²; allow sheet laps ≈ +10 % → use
  net area × 1.1 ÷ 14.4, round up.

## 4. Roof sheeting (SA profiles)

| Profile | Effective cover | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **IBR** (inverted box rib) | **686 mm** | industry standard; 0.3–0.8 mm gauge |
| Corrugated 10.5/76 | 762 mm | 8.5/76 = 610 mm |
| Widespan | 762 mm | |
| Big Six | 1016 mm | industrial |
| Grecca | 1100 mm | |

- End laps: **≥150 mm** (pitch > 15°) / **≥250 mm** (pitch ≤ 15°); side lap: one
  full corrugation.
- Slope area = plan footprint × pitch factor: flat/low (0–5°) ≈ **1.00**;
  moderate (10–17°) ≈ **1.05**; steep (26–35°) ≈ **1.15** (factor = 1/cos pitch).
- Add ≈ **+10 %** for laps and offcuts.
- Fasteners: 3 per sheet per purlin; Tek 65 mm (steel) / 90 mm (timber) + 25 mm
  bonded washer. Purlin spacing (steel, IBR): ≈1200 mm max per span tables.
- Count/measure separately: ridge capping (lin.), flashings (lin.), gutters
  (lin.), downpipes (No.), fascia/barge boards (lin.), insulation (m², under
  sheeting per SANS 204).

## 5. Wall plaster (SA)

- Mixes: exterior/exposed 1:3–1:4 (All Purpose / masonry cement); interior 1:4–1:6.
- Thickness: internal ≈10–12 mm; external ≈15–20 mm (max 15 mm/coat; 2 coats
  where >15 mm).
- Concrete Society SA (2024): **100 m² @ 15 mm = 700 kg cement (14 × 50 kg bags) +
  2.25 m³ sand** → per m²: **≈0.14 bag cement + 0.0225 m³ sand**.
- AfriSam Mix A coverage (2 × 50 kg bags + 4.5 wheelbarrows sand ≈0.25 m³ yield):
  10 mm → 24 m², 15 mm → 16 m², 20 mm → 12 m² (per batch).
- Formula: wet mortar = area × thickness; dry = wet × **1.33**; cement = dry/(1+r);
  sand = dry × r/(1+r); bags = cement m³ ÷ 0.033.
- Wastage: **+10 %**.
- Deduct openings **> 1 m²** each from plaster area.

---

## 6. Floor screed (SA)

- Bonded: min 25–30 mm; unbonded (over DPM): **50 mm**; floating: 65–75 mm.
- Mix: 1:3–1:4 sharp/coarse building sand; cement:sand.
- Volume = area × thickness; dry = wet × 1.33; split per ratio.
- 1:4 screed → ≈**8 × 50 kg bags cement + ≈0.8–1.0 m³ sand per m³ screed**.
- Wastage: +10 %.

## 8. DPC / DPM / waterproofing

- DPM (0.15–0.25 mm LDPE): to slab area + 10 % laps; laid on 50 mm sand blind.
- DPC strip: 300 mm wide, full wall lengths at ≈150 mm above finished ground.
- Waterproof below DPC brickwork/cavity trays at openings per drawings.

---

## 9. Excavation & founding (per ASAQS / SANS 1200)

- Strip footings: width ≈2 × wall width (typically 450–600 mm); depth to founding
  typically 600–1000 mm; m³ = continuous length × width × depth.
- Bulk excavation: m³ = area × depth.
- Backfill & compact: m³ = excavated volume − built volume (footing concrete).
- Filling under slab (sand/stone): 100–150 mm thin-batched = area × thickness.
- Measure as separate BOQ items (excavate / dispose / backfill) with units.

---

## 10. Doors / windows / lintels

- Count from the plan's window & door schedules (No. per size/type). Give each a
  BOQ item; state the schedule line item.
- Precast concrete lintels standard: 900/1200/1500/1800/2100/2400 mm;
  length = opening + 150 mm each side. Steel "NST" angle lintels for wide openings.
- Standard SA doors: internal 813 × 2032 mm (≈1.65 m²), 762 × 2032, 915 × 2032;
  external from schedule.

---

## 11. Painting (schedule/finish note only)

- Interior emulsion: ≈1 L per 8 m² per coat (2 coats specified);
- Exterior masonry: ≈1 L per 5 m² per coat.
- Painting is finish-driven; include only if noted on the plan/spec.

---

## 12. Plumbing & electrical

- NOT measurable from an architectural plan alone. Itemize as schedule-driven
  ("by schedule") with quantities only where an MEP sheet is provided.

---

## 13. Standard rules the agent must apply

1. Use these constraints exactly; show every formula and factor in the take-off.
2. Fewer guesses = better: never fill a missing dimension; ask.
3. Deduct openings: brick wall — net = gross − openings; plaster — openings
   >1 m² deducted.
4. Round quantities: counts (bricks, sheets, bags, tiles) **up** to whole units;
   volumes/areas to 2 decimals.
5. Add wastage only once, at the stated %.
6. End with a "verify before ordering" list (anything assumed or unreadable).
